About us:

Welcome to the Common Grounds coffee shop. We are a small, family run, independent business based in the heart of historic Lincoln. Our goal is to bring the experience of single origin coffees and their unique characteristics to all of our customers.

What are you looking for?

If you're looking for somewhere to relax and enjoy your drink or somewhere to sit and study, we have the perfect outdoor seating area for you. Enjoy a calm and quiet atmosphere whilst you try out a new drink or two.

If you're strugging to decide which drink to try out then consider taking our "What's your coffee?" quiz on our quiz page or head over to our review page to see what customers have said about their orders.
